Research

Understanding molecular spectra through the interactions that connect electronic, vibrational, rotational, and spin motion.

01 / VIBRONIC COUPLING

Beyond the Born–Oppenheimer picture

I study Jahn–Teller and pseudo-Jahn–Teller coupling and their signatures in molecular spectroscopy. Symmetry-adapted quasi-diabatic Hamiltonians provide a framework for describing interacting electronic states and nuclear motion.

02 / SPIN & VIBRATION

Spin dynamics in a moving molecule

My interests include spin–vibronic interactions and spin–phonon relaxation: how coupling to molecular and lattice motion influences spin states and their dynamics.

03 / COMPUTATIONAL METHODS

Models that meet computation

I develop computational methods for molecular spectroscopy, including machine-learned quasi-diabatic potential energy surfaces, direct Hamiltonian–vector products, and iterative techniques for large eigenvalue problems.
