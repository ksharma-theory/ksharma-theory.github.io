Overview

SOCJT is software for calculating spin-vibronic states of molecules with coupled electronic and vibrational motion, including systems that exhibit Born–Oppenheimer breakdown. It is developed by Ketan Sharma.

The current version is 4.0-beta. Earlier work and conference presentations refer to the software as SOCJT3.

Computational methods

  • Spin-vibronic eigenvectors for molecules with Jahn–Teller and pseudo-Jahn–Teller interactions.
  • Quasi-diabatic vibronic Hamiltonians and symmetry-based characterization of eigenstates.
  • Krylov–Schur diagonalization for large sparse eigenvalue problems.
  • Calculation and interpretation of molecular spectroscopic parameters.

Research applications

Related research includes the spin-vibronic spectrum of the methoxy radical, vibronic interactions in calcium alkoxides, and rotational signatures of Jahn–Teller distortions.
