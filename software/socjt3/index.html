SOCJT3

Vibronic and spin–vibronic calculations for molecular spectroscopy.

OverviewDownloadDocumentationPublications

Overview

SOCJT3 is my software for studying coupled electronic, vibrational, and spin degrees of freedom. Its computational approach uses direct Hamiltonian–vector products and iterative eigensolvers to address large vibronic problems.

Computational approach

  • Symmetry-adapted quasi-diabatic Hamiltonians
  • Direct Hamiltonian–vector multiplication
  • Lanczos and Krylov–Schur eigensolvers
  • Vibronic and spin–vibronic spectroscopy
